About PassUp.LK

Anyone Can Pass — that's not a slogan, it's the whole reason this platform exists.

Our Mission

PassUp.LK builds a premium Learning Management System for Sri Lankan ICT students, focused entirely on two syllabi: O/L ICT and A/L ICT. We believe every student deserves clear, structured, exam-focused teaching — not scattered YouTube videos and outdated notes.

Our Guarantee

If anyone fails even after following our program, we'll return 100% cashback. We only make that promise because the material and structure are built to work when followed properly.

How Enrollment Works

After signing up and browsing courses, you enroll and receive simple payment instructions. Once you send your payment slip via WhatsApp, our teacher personally verifies it and approves your access — usually within hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course? +

Sign up, verify your email, log in, browse courses, and click Enroll. You'll see payment instructions, send your slip via WhatsApp, and our teacher approves access — usually within a few hours.

What payment methods are accepted? +

Bank transfer to the account shown on the Payment Instructions page after enrolling. Send your payment slip via WhatsApp for verification.

How does the 100% cashback guarantee work? +

If you complete the full program as instructed and still fail, PassUp.LK refunds 100% of your course fee. Contact us after your results are released.

Can I access lessons on mobile? +

Yes — PassUp.LK is fully mobile-first and works on any modern phone browser.

Can I download the videos? +

Videos are streamed via protected YouTube embeds and are not downloadable. PDF notes attached to lessons can be downloaded.

Who do I contact for support? +

Use the Contact page or the WhatsApp number shown on your Payment Instructions page.

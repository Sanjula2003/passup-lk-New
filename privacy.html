Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your full name and email address via Supabase Authentication. When you enroll in a course, we store your enrollment status and any payment slip or reference you provide for manual verification.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to manage your account, verify enrollment payments, track your lesson progress, and communicate with you about your courses.

Data Storage

All data is stored securely in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase, protected by Row Level Security so only you and the course teacher can access your records.

Third Parties

Lesson videos are embedded from YouTube; PDF materials may be hosted on Google Drive. These services have their own privacy policies which apply when you interact with embedded content.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via our Contact page.
